Privacy Policy

EKO Solar Pros · Atlanta, Georgia · Last updated: August 1, 2026

EKO Solar Pros ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website ekosolarpros.com and provides solar repair, diagnostic, and troubleshooting services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or contacting us, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, request service, or opt in to text-message alerts, we may collect:

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Service details — the type of solar service you need and any message or description you provide.
  • Mobile number and messaging consent — if you or an authorized staff member opt in to our SMS alert program.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule solar diagnostic and repair services.
  • To send operational and service-related notifications, including appointment updates and payment confirmations.
  • To send text-message (SMS) alerts to individuals who have opted in, as described in our SMS Terms & Conditions.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our services.

SMS / Text Messaging Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile opt-in information, phone number, or SMS consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information collected through the SMS program is used solely to deliver the messages you have opted in to receive and to operate the messaging program.

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for full details.

How We Share Information

We may share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, our messaging and payment providers), and only to the extent necessary to provide those services. These providers are bound to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and we use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
