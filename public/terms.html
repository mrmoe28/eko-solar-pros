SMS Terms & Conditions

EKO Solar Pros · Atlanta, Georgia · Last updated: August 1, 2026

These Terms & Conditions govern the EKO Solar Pros SMS (text message) program. By opting in, you agree to these terms.

Program Description

EKO Solar Pros operates an SMS alert program that sends operational and service-related notifications — new service requests, appointment confirmations and updates, deposit and invoice payment confirmations, and payment-failure alerts — to the business owner and authorized staff regarding solar service work. This is not a marketing or promotional program.

How to Opt In

Authorized users may opt in to receive text messages in either of these ways:

  • From the authenticated EKO Solar Ops application, on the Settings → SMS Alerts page, by entering their own mobile number and enabling the alert toggles, or
  • By texting START to (404) 738-5722.

Only the individual who controls the enrolled mobile number receives messages. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your account activity and service events.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. EKO Solar Pros does not charge for the messages, but your mobile carrier's standard message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out & Help

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages, unless you re-enroll.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (404) 738-5722.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers may change without notice.

Privacy

Your mobile opt-in information and consent are never sold or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your information.

Contact Us

Call or text (404) 738-5722, or reach us through the contact form at ekosolarpros.com.
